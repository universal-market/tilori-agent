### otherHome
The Universal Market plugin's options serve the default Tilori home, and this is /home/example/.config/tilori-other. Start this home's sessions with `sh ~/.claude/plugins/data/universal-market-tilori-claude/tilori-agent claude --home /home/example/.config/tilori-other` instead; it passes this home's URLs to Claude Code.
